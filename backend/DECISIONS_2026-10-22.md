# Owner decisions, 2026-10-22 (single source for Phase 3)
1. FIXED PRICE is the way to buy in the early website. FR-29 ("no Buy Now; negotiation only") is REVERSED. Offers/negotiation are DEFERRED: dormant behind a feature flag (OFFERS_ENABLED, default off), tables and code kept, UI hides them. Orders start (Phase 4) from a Buy action that reserves the listing with a payment window; orders.accepted_offer_id must stop being required in Phase 4. Commission applies to the price (FR-46a unchanged).
2. Messaging stays (FR-28): login required, tied to a published listing, no self-conversation; read-only once the listing is sold/removed. Contact details (phone, email, link) are BLOCKED with a clear error; blocked text is NOT stored, only an attempt counter; prices/measurements ("PKR 85,000", "85000", "chest 40 length 42") must not be blocked. Conversations are readable by the two participants only; staff only with a dispute/report reference, and every staff read is logged in an audit table.
3. Price edits on a published listing (FR-73e): a decrease up to platform_settings.instant_price_decrease_max_percent (default 30) applies immediately; larger decreases and ALL increases go through moderation. Strictly-above-threshold email verification (high_value_threshold_pkr) is unchanged. The buyer pays the price confirmed at checkout (live price check + price-change confirmation; price locked at reservation, Phase 4).
4. Phase 3 Buy button: login-gated (redirect-and-return after login), leads to a clear "checkout opens soon" screen. No reservation or payment before Phase 4.
5. Chat updates by polling: ~15 s in an open chat, 60 s for inbox/unread (configurable). No real-time.
6. Message notifications: in-app plus a throttled email through the existing email adapter/dev outbox: only if unread 15 minutes, at most one email per conversation per hour.
7. Per-account limits: 10 conversations/hour and 30/day; 10 messages/minute and 60/hour; messages max 1000 characters (all configurable).
8. Search hides sold items; reserved items show a "Reserved" badge. Sold page: 200 + "Sold" banner + similar items (same sub-category, price within 30%, newest six) + noindex. Paused: 200 "temporarily unavailable" + noindex. Removed by seller or taken down by a moderator: 410 Gone.
9. English and Roman Urdu search; English-only interface. Urdu script and right-to-left are DEFERRED.
10. Suggestions (FR-80c minimal): worker-built versioned vocabulary file (categories, brands, fabrics, colors, synonyms) matched in the browser, plus browser-only recent searches. NO trending, NO "did you mean".
11. Filters: main category, sub-category/occasion, condition, price range, city (= the SELLER's profile city), plus color, brand, fabric. Sorts: newest, price low->high, price high->low, relevance (only with a query). Default: relevance with a query, else newest. Popularity DEFERRED.
12. Color/brand/fabric: admin-managed lists; sellers pick from a list or type a custom value; a custom value joins the filter only after admin approval (approval queue, proper new permission).
13. Staleness: listing detail/status up to 10 s; search/category pages up to 60 s. No version pointers/caching proposals (FR-73b not adopted).
14. SEO: the system RECOMMENDS a title/description built from structured fields (condition, sub-category, color, fabric, brand, city); the seller sees it labeled as a recommendation and may accept it or write their own. Guardrails: "Replica" is always enforced in the title for replica listings and own text cannot call a replica original/authentic; title <= 60 and description <= ~155 characters; no all-caps/excess punctuation; contact-info filter applies; moderators review it in normal listing approval and edits go through revisions; NO price in the description. Structured data: Product + Offer (price, PKR, availability, condition, brand, color) + BreadcrumbList; home WebSite/Organization; NO star ratings.
15. URLs: /listing/<slug>-<shortid>; the ID is the key; old slugs redirect; canonical tags. Indexable: home, main categories, sub-categories, category+city. NOINDEX: search results and any page with price/sort/condition/color/brand/fabric filters. Worker-generated sitemap and robots.txt.
16. Snapshots (FR-81a): worker-generated HTML for home, categories, sub-categories, category+city and listings, stored in Postgres, regenerated through the queue on publish, price change, sold, removal and revision approval; the same HTML is served to everyone (no cloaking); the SPA takes over. Production serving model is a LIVE_CHECK.
17. CDN: build CDN-ready (immutable long-lived headers on hashed media, short page caching, a purge adapter + fake). Provider chosen before launch (LIVE_CHECK).
18. Frontend: public JS ~150-200 KB compressed enforced in the build; self-hosted trimmed fonts (font-display: swap); route-level code splitting; no admin code for public visitors; the design-reference/ theme (deep green/cream/gold, Playfair Display + DM Sans) is final.
19. Draft static pages (About, How it works, Terms, Privacy, Help, Contact): marked "Draft", noindexed, NO refund/return/payment promises. Final legal text is a pre-launch item.
Provisional defaults: home = category tiles + newest published listings; no paid featured slots; the API/UI say "price" while the asking_price column keeps its name; city verification is required at Buy (Phase 4), not for messaging.
Deferred (END_OF_PHASES_REVIEW): offers/negotiation, Urdu script/RTL, SMS/WhatsApp/push, trending + "did you mean", CDN provider, final legal text, popularity sort, real-time chat.
